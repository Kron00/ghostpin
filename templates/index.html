Ghostpin

Put your iPhone anywhere on the map. The change is system-wide, so most apps follow it. Games and banking apps often run extra checks GPS alone won't pass.

Step 1 of 3

Turn on Developer Mode

Your iPhone only accepts a simulated location with this on. It's a built-in Apple setting and safe to enable.

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone
  2. Go to Privacy & Security
  3. Scroll down and tap Developer Mode
  4. Switch it on and restart when prompted
Don't see Developer Mode? It appears on iOS 16 and later.
Step 2 of 3

Connect and trust

Plug your iPhone into this computer with a cable.

  1. Connect with a USB cable (Lightning or USB-C)
  2. Unlock your iPhone
  3. Tap Trust when “Trust This Computer?” appears
  4. Enter your passcode to confirm
No prompt? Replug the cable, or reset it in Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Reset Location & Privacy.
Step 3 of 3

You're all set

A few ways to get around:

  • Click the map to drop a pin, then press Warp
  • Turn on Teleport to move with a single click
  • Search for any address, place, or coordinate
  • Shift-click several points to travel along a route
  • Press G for the consistency guide and app risk levels
On recent iOS versions, keep your iPhone connected while spoofing — Ghostpin holds the location for as long as it's plugged in.

What Ghostpin does for you
✓Real road routes. Movement follows actual streets at a set speed, not a straight line.
✓Adaptive speed. Optional; follows posted limits and eases off at junctions.
✓Cooldown timer. Warns you when a jump would look like impossible travel. It doesn't block the jump — that's your call.
✓Speed randomization. Optional and off by default; varies pace along a route.
✓IP mismatch alert. Warns when your connection says one place and your GPS says another.

Ghostpin doesn't add GPS jitter. Reasserting a slightly different pin every second makes a stationary spoof visibly wobble in Maps, and on recent iOS it can read as two competing location sources — worse than sitting still.

What you should do
1Use a VPN in your spoofed region for streaming and banking
2Change your time zone: Settings → General → Date & Time, turn off Set Automatically
3Move naturally — use routes and roaming instead of teleporting
4Respect the cooldown — wait until it reads Safe
5Avoid rapid teleports — apps flag impossible travel
App risk levels
Low: Maps, Weather, social, dating, delivery
Moderate: Uber, Lyft, Snapchat, Life360 — use routes
High: Pokémon Go, banking apps — use a VPN and the cooldown
What GPS spoofing can't hide
×CLLocation metadata such as speed and altitude — an Apple API limit
×Motion sensors — the accelerometer shows the phone is still
×Wi-Fi and Bluetooth fingerprinting used by banking fraud SDKs
×Cell tower triangulation by your carrier

Most consumer apps don't check these. Banking and gaming apps may.

Ways to build a route
StopsAn ordered itinerary: A to B, with as many stops between as you like. Fill each one from the search bar or by pointing at the map, drag the letters to reorder, and tick Return to the start to close the loop. Calculate route then follows real roads.
RoamNo path at all. Pick a centre and a radius, and the location wanders at random inside that area, changing direction as it goes. Good for looking like you're somewhere without going anywhere in particular.
MapLay out a route by pointing. Each click adds the next stop, so you can trace a whole journey without typing. Press Esc when you're done. These stops behave exactly like searched ones, so the two can be mixed.
